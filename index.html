📐 النظريات الأساسية

Rolle's Theorem:
If f(a)=f(b), ∃c∈(a,b): f'(c)=0
MVT:
f'(c) = [f(b)-f(a)]/(b-a)
Conditions:
Continuous on [a,b], Differentiable on (a,b)
